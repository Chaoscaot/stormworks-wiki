# Part families

The wiki now has a deterministic editorial family dataset in `wiki/data/families.json`, generated by `wiki/scripts/extract_families.py`. This pass changed only those files and this report. It reads the existing block catalogue and the supplied definition XML. It does not access the shared native REA session or execute the game.

The dataset contains 536 families for 759 definitions. There are 93 explicitly curated families, including 89 with multiple variants, and 443 conservative singleton fallbacks. Every definition occurs in exactly one family's variant list and has exactly one `blockToFamily` entry. There are 8,641 source findings covering all 759 definition files.

## Reproduction

Run from the project root:

```bash
python wiki/scripts/extract_families.py
python wiki/scripts/extract_families.py --check
```

The script locates its inputs relative to its own location, or accepts `--root`. It uses the standard library and the existing definition parser in `extract_blocks.py`. That parser normalizes the known numeric XML rotation-matrix attribute names in memory. Source hashes always cover the original bytes.

Output has no timestamps or absolute machine paths. `--check` regenerates the document in memory and compares bytes without writing the dataset. Import bytecode writing is disabled. The extractor checks the original definition inventory, duplicate assignment, unknown members, missing comparison values, source hashes, source attributes, source tooltips, port counts and attributes, numeric mass/value, and voxel counts. A missing catalogue value is omitted from that family's comparison fields rather than invented. The five Air-Liquid members have an explicit membership check.

## Schema and UI contract

The top-level keys are `families`, `blockToFamily` and `summary`, with `summary.schemaVersion` equal to 1.

Each family contains `id`, `title`, `category`, `summary`, `body`, `sections`, `variants`, `comparisonFields`, `findings`, `sources` and `limitations`. Sections have `id`, `title` and `body`. Variants have `id` and `sizeLabel`; `id` is the existing block ID, and `sizeLabel` retains the full original component display name. Family IDs are stable editorial slugs, independent of numeric game category values. Fallback IDs begin with `part-`.

Comparison fields have `key`, `label`, `unit` and `meaning`. Resolve `key` as a dot path against the block selected by `variant.id`. The standard paths are `mass`, `value` and `physics.voxels.count`. Selected families also use `attributes.electric_charge_capacity`, `attributes.electric_magnitude`, `attributes.pump_pressure` or `attributes.force_emitter_max_force`. All fields resolve to actual non-null values for every member of the family. There is no separate comparison-value matrix. `unit` is null for these fields because this pass does not establish their units. Port descriptions independently retain explicit telemetry units such as L/s, litres and atmospheres.

Findings have `label`, `value`, `detail`, `evidenceId`, `status` and `source`. Source records include the relative `path`, full original-file `sha256`, and inclusive one-based `lineStart` and `lineEnd`. Stable location IDs have the form `resource:rom/data/definitions/air_exchanger.xml:4122d92d2e85:L129-L129`. The 12-digit digest prefix in the ID is accompanied by the full SHA-256 in `source`. Findings that share a source tag can share a location ID. These are file-evidence IDs, not retained native REA session IDs. `observed` means present in the supplied file, not observed during gameplay.

The family `sources` array lists original definition identities. `summary.inputs` records the exact catalogue hash. Individual findings retain each member's own tooltip and port wording, including empty-description port labels and conflicting descriptions. Render the article body and sections as the general overview and put `limitations` in one limitations section. The curated heat-exchanger overview, connections and sizes use direct encyclopedia prose; evidence and research qualifications stay in findings and limitations.

## Air-Liquid Heat Exchanger

The `air-liquid-heat-exchanger` family contains all five definitions, in comparison order:

- `air_exchanger`, Air-Liquid Heat Exchanger 1x2, mass 2, value 15, 2 listed voxels.
- `air_exchanger_5_2`, Air-Liquid Heat Exchanger 5x2, mass 7, value 30, 10 listed voxels.
- `air_exchanger_5_3`, Air-Liquid Heat Exchanger 5x3, mass 45, value 50, 45 listed voxels.
- `air_exchanger_9_3`, Air-Liquid Heat Exchanger 9x3, mass 81, value 80, 81 listed voxels.
- `air_exchanger_9_5`, Air-Liquid Heat Exchanger 9x5, mass 225, value 150, 225 listed voxels.

Each has Air In, Air Out, Fluid In, Fluid Out, Temp A and Temp B. Temp A is compartment air temperature. Temp B is compartment water temperature, although the general tooltip calls the second medium a liquid. These descriptions identify compartment readings, not inlet/outlet temperature probes. The supplied tooltip says heat is averaged between air and liquid at a rate proportional to component size. The article explains the two circuits and compares the five variants without assigning a numerical exchange rate.

## Other curated families

Liquid-Liquid Heat Exchanger groups `intercooler` and `intercooler_large`, named 2x2 and 5x5. Their masses are 4 and 16, values 30 and 50, and listed voxel counts 4 and 25. Both have separate A/B inlet and outlet pairs and compartment temperature readings.

Air-Air Heat Exchanger groups three definitions under their display sizes 2x2, 2x5 and 3x9. Two filenames contain different numbers, so filename suffixes do not supply the article's size labels. Passive Fluid Heat Radiator is separate from the two Electric Fluid Heat Radiator variants. Only the electric radiator description says electricity for the fan increases exchange rate.

Other substantive articles cover batteries, generators, self-contained diesel engines, electric motors, electric fluid pumps, mechanically driven impeller pumps, fluid tanks, gas tanks, marine propellers and pitchable marine propellers. Batteries expose internal capacity values 1600, 12800 and 256000 and a 0-to-1 charge output. Pump pressure fields of 10 and 100 remain unitless, while Flow Rate ports explicitly say L/s. Tank telemetry uses litres and atmospheres without inventing a capacity from bounds.

Rotors are separated by documented controls and connection arrangements. Heavy rotors, conventional helicopter rotors, parts with two mechanical power connections and terminal parts are separate families. Aircraft propellers and tail rotors remain separate. Gearbox size variants are separate from legacy gearbox definitions; the stated torque-difference limit of 4000 is restricted to `torque_gearbox_2`.

Modular engine cylinders, crankshafts, converters, drive belts, clutches, flywheels, exhaust manifolds and manifold extensions each have their own families. Air, coolant and fuel manifolds, starters, alternators and belt-driven pumps retain separate singleton articles rather than being merged by the modular-engine prefix. Cylinder composite values 1, 2 and 3 retain their documented air-volume, fuel-volume and temperature meanings.

Reviewed structural series include wedge, pyramid and inverse-pyramid building shapes; flat, angled, diamond, pyramid, inverse-pyramid, corner and porthole windows; pipes and enclosed pipes. Static root blocks and weight blocks are separate. Weapons preserve caliber and attachment roles. Wheels preserve suspension and tank drive roles. Paired multibody definitions and legacy/new radios are conservative singleton fallbacks because name similarity alone does not establish interchangeability.

## Limits and validation

No native behavior, measured performance, heat-transfer coefficients, effectiveness, torque curves, power ratings or internal-unit conversions were recovered. Common serialized defaults do not establish active mechanics. Family membership and category are editorial. Singleton coverage provides navigation without asserting that unreviewed similarly named parts are equivalent.

Generation and `--check` pass. An independent validation checks all 759 unique memberships and reverse mappings, every comparison path, exact requested Air-Liquid order and comparison values, schema keys, full source hashes, evidence line ranges and the evidence-ID format. The report and JSON preserve the distinction between intended interfaces and runtime implementation.
