# Static followups for Stormworks

Target `stormworks64.exe`, SHA-256 `f9206d85c82f4d02fd0ac391781d19c5c68394a9ffc48accca0e1f5966db8699`. Static analysis only. The executable was never run. No SI conversions are attempted. Instruction evidence and search inventories are retained in `native/followups/`.

A verdict of partly means that static work remains. It does not mean that running the game is necessary. A bounded absence search is not a proof of executable-wide absence.

## 1. Power Add and Power Meter, #8

Verdict: answered for the shipped definitions' active calculation. These legacy gates do not add power or calculate a meter reading. They retain a passive shaft-node connection. Calling the entire component unconnected or proving that its shaft state is always zero would be incorrect.

The important correction is in the shared port lookup `0x1403224f0`. Its mode filter is disabled for mechanical, fluid and electrical port types. At `0x1403224fc`, `41 8d 41 fe`, it forms `type - 2`; `0x140322503`, `83 f8 02`, and `0x140322509`, `0f 97 44 24 68`, enable mode filtering only outside the inclusive type interval `[2,4]`. At `0x140322670`, `80 7c 24 68 00`, and `0x140322675`, `74 0b`, a disabled filter skips the mode comparison at `0x14032267b`, `43 39 4c 29 30`. Thus the binder's input-mode argument does not exclude the mode-zero mechanical output in either XML file.

Both definitions have just one mechanical port. The binder resolves mechanical ordinals into the definition pointers at component `+0x2c0`, `+0x310` and `+0x5b0`; only the first can resolve for these definitions. The stores are `0x1403c3849`, `48 89 87 c0 02 00 00`; `0x1403c386d`, `48 89 87 10 03 00 00`; and `0x1403c397c`, `48 89 87 b0 05 00 00`. Registration `0x1403c2fd0` places the corresponding mechanical nodes in the shared component list, and shaft lookup `0x1404c2550` accepts registered nodes with a non-null definition pointer. The lookup's port-mode exception, rather than an unlocated special transmission implementation, explains how a legacy output can still be a shaft member. See `pseudocode/1403c2fd0.c`, `pseudocode/1404c2550.c`, and `asm/shaft-lookup-filter.asm`.

There is no second resolved mechanical port, reciprocal bridge, active submission formula or numeric measurement output in either shipped definition. The gate tick loads the type at `0x1403c4e88`, `48 63 81 70 0e 00 00`, and dispatches through `0x1403c4e9f`, `8b 84 82 54 95 3c 00`, followed by `0x1403c4ea9`, `ff e0`. The type entries at `0x1403c95b4`, `f2 94 3c 00`, and `0x1403c95b8`, `f2 94 3c 00`, both select the common exit `0x1403c94f2`. These bytes are also checked against the supplied executable, rather than inferred from the XML names.

The generic shaft code may publish a connected group's state into the passive node. That is not an addition or measurement operation performed by either gate. No sum, torque-times-speed or weighted-speed formula should be attributed to these blocks. The external type-use inventory and full gate vtable are retained in `tools/gate-type-whole.txt` and `tools/gate-vtable-full.txt`; mechanical registration, lookup and binding evidence is retained under `asm/` and `pseudocode/`. The untyped whole-binary search is a coverage aid, not a formal proof about arbitrary corrupted or injected component state.

What stays open: no running-game measurement is needed to establish the missing active calculation. Display behavior for arbitrary saved legacy property values and the behavior of a particular connected shaft assembly are separate questions.

Wiki-ready replacement: "Power Add and Power Meter are legacy gates with one passive mechanical connection. In this build neither gate performs a power addition or meter calculation, although its mechanical port can still join a shaft network."

## 4. Plain and power pivot quarter-turn stop, #9

Verdict: answered for the angular constraint. These flag-64 pivots do not receive a quarter-turn hinge limit. The deferred physics constructor also starts with its angular limit disabled, so it does not supply a hidden default quarter-turn stop.

The XML evidence is retained in `tools/definition-summary.txt`. Both requested parent definitions have flags `64` and range-of-motion `0.5`. The setup creates a hinge through `0x1401a69e0` at `0x140357860`, `e8 7b f1 e4 ff`. Its limit-setting arm requires flag `0x100` and excludes flag `0x200`: `0x140357877`, `48 f7 c1 00 01 00 00`, and `0x140357880`, `48 f7 c1 00 02 00 00`. These definitions do not satisfy that arm. The actual setter call is `0x1403578be`, `e8 8d 14 e5 ff`, targeting `0x1401a8d50`.

This pass follows the deferred creation instead of stopping at the wrapper. The wrapper's command vtable at `0x140b6ae58`, `30 0c 1a 40 01 00 00 00`, selects `0x1401a0c30`. Its shared constructor block installs the hinge's default angular-limit state:

- `0x14019f1a7`, `45 33 e4`, establishes zero. `0x14019f2d7`, `4c 89 a3 80 05 00 00`, stores the zero center at constraint `+0x580`.
- `0x14019f2de`, `48 b8 00 00 00 00 00 00 f0 bf`, constructs double `-1`; `0x14019f2e8`, `48 89 83 88 05 00 00`, stores the negative half-range at `+0x588`.
- The solver-side test `0x140125620` loads that half-range at `0x14012563d`, `f2 0f 10 b3 88 05 00 00`, compares it with zero at `0x140125648`, `66 0f 2f f1`, and skips the angular-limit calculation on the negative result at `0x140125660`, `0f 82 93 00 00 00`.

The decoded rule is `angular_stop_enabled = half_range >= 0`. Default `half_range = -1` therefore means no enabled stop, not a quarter turn. `pseudocode/140125620.c` contains the corresponding solver test; `asm/1401a0c30.asm` retains the constructor's shared block. `tools/hinge-limit-setter-callers.txt` inventories the direct setter callers. The requested joint class has only the setup call above; the other callers belong to other component implementations.

What stays open: collision-limited travel of a particular assembled vehicle depends on its surrounding geometry. That is not a universal angular-stop constant. A claim about actual travel under a specified load and collision arrangement would require a physics simulation or the running game. The static hinge configuration itself is settled.

Wiki-ready replacement: "Plain and power pivots do not receive the tooltip's quarter-turn angular stop in this build. Their hinge starts with the angular limit disabled, although surrounding vehicle geometry can still obstruct movement."

## 6. Connector electricity and signal latency, #141

Verdict: answered for the normal native attachment and scalar/composite signal path. Small, hinge and large connectors do not create an electrical bridge across their attachment. Their electrical ports supply the magnet locally. The link copies signals during the component update and publishes them after all component updates, so receiving logic uses them on its next update.

The shipped definitions identify large, small and hinge as connector subtypes 0, 1 and 2, respectively. These are attachment switch entries, not the component class ID. The switch loads definition subtype at `0x1403d1cec`, `48 63 83 7c 0e 00 00`, indexes the table at `0x1403d1d03`, `8b 8c 82 fc 40 3d 00`, and jumps at `0x1403d1d0d`, `ff e1`. The corresponding table entries are `0x1403d40fc`, `0f 1d 3d 00`; `0x1403d4100`, `ca 26 3d 00`; and `0x1403d4104`, `fa 27 3d 00`. Their complete branches are retained in `asm/connector-attach-final.asm` and `pseudocode/1403d19f0.c`.

Electrical membership is separate from the mechanical peer pointer. Connector registration puts its electrical node at component `+0x908`: `0x1403d0a71`, `48 8d 9f 08 09 00 00`. Its bridge is node `+0x30`, hence component `+0x938`. Construction initializes that bridge to null at `0x1403d05a8`, `48 89 bb 38 09 00 00`, using the constructor's zero register. The attachment's reciprocal electrical setters are restricted to the electric subtype and the hardpoint branch. The electric-subtype test is `0x1403d27b8`, `83 bb 7c 0e 00 00 04`; its setters are `0x1403d27cf`, `48 89 87 38 09 00 00`, and `0x1403d27dd`, `48 89 81 38 09 00 00`. The hardpoint setters are `0x1403d4059`, `48 89 87 38 09 00 00`, and `0x1403d4067`, `48 89 81 38 09 00 00`. Small bypasses the electric-subtype arm, while large and hinge take different switch branches. None installs those bridges.

`tools/electrical-link-offset-inventory.txt` records the whole-disassembly offset inventory; its connector-class subset has only constructor clearing, release clearing and those attachment setters. The electrical pool traversal in `pseudocode/1404c9d20.c` follows an electrical node's bridge and its group, not the mechanical peer. Thus electrical demand from a small, hinge or large magnet does not prove that electricity crosses its link. There is no cross-link conductance, capacity or loss formula to assign to these three native attachments. This conclusion concerns the attachment itself; a separate cable or another electrical connection in an assembly can still join its electrical networks.

The signal operation is a direct copy to the peer's output cache. The Boolean load/store is `0x1403d4365`, `0f b6 83 d0 01 00 00`, and `0x1403d436c`, `88 81 28 02 00 00`. The ordinary numeric load/store is `0x1403d4393`, `f3 0f 10 83 60 02 00 00`, and `0x1403d439b`, `f3 0f 11 81 80 02 00 00`. Composite copying begins at `0x1403d43b1`, `8b 83 b8 02 00 00`, selects the peer payload at `0x1403d43b7`, `48 81 c1 58 03 00 00`, and copies the remaining payload with the vector loads/stores retained in `tools/composite-copy-exact.txt`. This path has no FIFO, interpolation or averaging.

The missing scheduling connection is now resolved. Scene `0x140549240` invokes the component update slot at `0x1405498d6`, `41 ff 92 58 03 00 00`. Its outer loop finishes at `0x140549914`, `0f 82 e6 fd ff ff`, before the subsequent output-publication loop. Publication invokes each output port's slot at `0x140549ad2`, `ff 50 28`. For an already connected, normally updated pair, let `A[n]` be the connector input cache sampled during component update `n`, `B[n]` the peer output after that update, and `R[n]` the directly wired receiver's input during its update. The recovered recurrence is `B[n] = A[n]` and `R[n+1] = B[n]`. There is no extra queue inside the link; there is one component-update boundary between the connector's sampled input and receiving logic using the value. If an ordinary upstream source first calculates a new output during update `n`, it publishes that to the connector after the updates; the connector copies it during `n+1`, and ordinary downstream logic uses it during `n+2`. These integer offsets follow from the two ordered scene loops and the cache stores above, not a wall-clock measurement or an assumption about vehicle iteration order.

What stays open: this is scalar/composite logic timing for active vehicles with an established link. Connection establishment, sleeping/unloaded vehicles, network replication and rendered video frame latency are separate scheduling questions. A specified multiplayer or video wall-clock delay requires runtime measurement of that setup. No running-game measurement is needed for the native electrical-bridge decision or the active scalar/composite recurrence.

Wiki-ready replacement: "Small, hinge and large connectors use electricity for their magnets but do not bridge electricity across the attachment. Signals copy to the peer's output during the component update and reach receiving logic on its next update, with no additional queue inside the link."

For exchange, use signed transferred jet fuel `dJ`, positive toward the private buffer. The two branch stores implement `J' = max(0, J + dJ)` and a corresponding connected-container subtraction. `dJ` comes from the donor's species fraction and the signed transfer amount, not an afterburner burn rate. The exhaust requests every species with mask `0xffffffff` at `0x140351d6b`, `c7 44 24 30 ff ff ff ff`; its pressure argument is `-10`, loaded at `0x140351d81`, `f3 0f 10 15 0b f2 85 00`, from `0x140bb0f94`, `00 00 20 c1`. Actual exchange remains conditional on a bound network and its state.

The mixing writer is worth distinguishing from combustion. Let `S_P` and `S_N` be selected-species totals in the private and network containers, `N` the network's jet fuel, and `m` the helper's exchange amount capped to both selected totals. When both totals exceed binary32 `0.0001`, it removes `a = m*J/S_P` and `b = m*N/S_N`, then adds `(a+b)*0.5` to each container. Thus `J' = J + 0.5*m*(N/S_N - J/S_P)`, with the arithmetic performed in the instruction order shown in `asm/jet-diffusion-final.asm`. It conserves combined jet fuel apart from rounding; it does not destroy fuel as an afterburner burn would. The total checks load the threshold at `0x1402ed4c1`, `f3 0f 10 05 cf 23 8c 00`, constant `0x140baf898`, `17 b7 d1 38`. The cap uses `0x1402ed4ea`, `f3 0f 5d c3`, and `0x1402ed4ee`, `f3 0f 5d e8`; the half factor is loaded at `0x1402ed4e2`, `f3 0f 10 35 d2 27 8c 00`, from `0x140bafcbc`, `00 00 00 3f`. The caller supplies the original species mask through `0x1402eca17`, `8b 84 24 00 01 00 00`, and `0x1402ed33c`, `44 8b c8`. The intervening `0x1402ec210` preserves that register; its jump table only accumulates a port statistic. Ghidra's inferred return value for that helper must not be mistaken for a newly calculated species mask.

The exhaust's local use remains a read for thrust scaling. With afterburner enabled and positive stored total, the decoded multiplier is `1 + (J/0.001)*0.5`, equivalently `1 + 500*J` in real arithmetic. The binary32 constants and original operation order are authoritative. The fuel read/divide/multiply/add are `0x140351eab`, `f3 0f 10 97 cc 03 00 00`; `0x140351eb3`, `f3 41 0f 5e d3`; `0x140351eb8`, `f3 0f 59 15 fc dd 85 00`; and `0x140351ec0`, `f3 41 0f 58 d5`. The divisor is loaded at `0x140351ddb`, `f3 44 0f 10 1d 14 db 85 00`, from `0x140baf8f8`, `6f 12 83 3a`; the additive one comes from `0x140351500`, `f3 44 0f 10 2d c3 e9 85 00`, constant `0x140bafecc`, `00 00 80 3f`. No local `J -= burn` follows this multiplier.

What stays open: `tools/container-alias-whole.txt` inventories literal address formation and `tools/buffer-whole.txt` inventories direct-offset matches with unwind owners. They do not cover a pointer formed by multiple additions, a pointer loaded from another object, a write through an interior pointer retained elsewhere, or a generic bulk copy whose range overlaps this component. The current work follows the private alias through the demonstrated exhaust calls, including the newly mapped mixing callee, but is not a whole-program points-to proof for all external consumers of a jet-component pointer. The same-offset candidates in other classes must not be promoted to jet writers without a valid receiver-type path. That precise universal exclusion remains static work. Running the game could measure a particular network's net fuel flow, but cannot prove that no other writer exists in the executable or distinguish combustion from mixing without observing these states.
