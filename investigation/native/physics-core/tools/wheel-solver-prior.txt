157:Bodies can override gravity. `0x1401a75f0` writes a scalar at wrapper `+0x10` and queues an event; `0x1401a1ad0` sets the body's gravity acceleration to `(0,-g,0)` and updates its mass-scaled gravity force. The body-build call at `0x1404a9595..0x1404a95a8` uses that vehicle field. `0x1404be190` can redistribute vehicle mass and write `+0x730` at `0x1404be8aa`; the complete redistribution rule is not summarized as a universal constant. This rules out claiming every vehicle part always accelerates at the bare world setting.
