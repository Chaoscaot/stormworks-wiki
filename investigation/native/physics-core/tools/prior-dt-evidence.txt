11:- Rigid-body physics has a separate substep. The worker passes either `double(float(1)/physics_rate)` or the fallback `double(float(1/60))` to the physics solver. The coordinator contains 60, 120 and 180 settings. Those are solver rates, not three possible multipliers for the recovered component formulas.
12-- Both `server64.exe` and `server.exe` contain the same tick-count/60 call pattern. Shipped Lua code also converts a callback interval of N ticks to N/60 seconds.
13-- Multiply amounts per update by 60. For a constant relaxation fraction `k`, compound the recurrence: a gap retains `(1-k)^60` after one simulated second. This establishes simulation-time conversion, not a guarantee of 60 updates per wall-clock second under every load or game mode.

100-## The accumulator and solver detail
101-
102-The called routine implements a fixed-substep accumulator [A]:
103-
104-1. `0x140133fd6`, `f2 0f 58 b3 00 02 00 00`, adds stored world `+0x200` to elapsed.
105-2. `0x140133fde`, `f2 0f 11 bb 08 02 00 00`, stores the supplied substep at world `+0x208`.
106-3. `0x140133ff7`, `f2 0f 5e c7`, divides accumulated elapsed by substep. `0x140133ffb`, `f2 0f 2c e8`, truncates the quotient to a substep count.
107-4. `0x140134007` multiplies that count by substep; `0x14013400b`, `f2 0f 5c f1`, subtracts it from the accumulator.
108-5. The count is capped against the supplied maximum at `0x14013409b-0x14013409f`. At `0x140134152`, `0f 28 cf`, the substep is placed in XMM1; `0x140134158`, `ff 90 38 01 00 00`, calls the single-step virtual method. The loop repeats at `0x140134170`.
109-
110-The component-dispatch call is outside this accumulator. Inferring the component rate from the solver's internalSingleStep calls would give the wrong multiplier when physics detail changes.
111-
112:The coordinator's automatic solver-rate branch stores 60 at `0x140743954`, 180 at `0x140743968`, and 120 at `0x140743987` [S]. Their immediate binary32 encodings are `00 00 70 42`, `00 00 34 43`, and `00 00 f0 42`. It also accepts a nonzero rate from a context field, so the three automatic choices do not establish a complete list of allowed overrides. The `physics_timestep` string at `0x140b10a58` is bound to a settings field at `+0x40` by `0x1402765d9-0x1402765f0` [stormworks64-settings-binding.asm]. This report does not claim to have traced every setting-copy operation or identified the global branch flag `0x140ceb6ff` by name.

114-## Do the recovered formulas receive a dt?
115-
116-The solver timestep is computed inside the physics worker after the component pass. It is not forwarded from that worker into `F_FLUID`, the electric update, or the modular-engine component/group updates. Their recovered increments already describe a normal component update.
117-
118-- `F_FLUID 0x1403ef860` is reached through the component slot above. The scoop constructs bias and factor, then calls `0x1402ec710` with those quantities; they are not time arguments. The port routine computes demand, clamps the allocated move, and transfers amounts directly. Its full body is retained in `stormworks64-port-exchange.asm` and `pseudocode/0x1402ec710.c`.
119-- The radiator multiplies its airflow term by the fixed relaxation coefficient at `0x1403f3c4f`, `f3 0f 59 35 c1 bc 7b 00`, then passes that fraction to the interpolation helper at `0x1403f3c68`, `e8 43 c8 4f 00`. There is no additional elapsed-time multiplier in that operation [stormworks64-radiator-relaxation.asm; cooling-model.md].
120-- The motor passes the computed charge request to `0x1402eeca0` at `0x140400a20`, `e8 7b e2 ee ff`. This helper consumes the node pointer and request, applies battery availability, and returns the available fraction. It does not scale the request by a dt [stormworks64-electric-draw.asm, stormworks64-electric-update.asm].
121-- The generator deposits `Q` charge per component update and sets its output to `60*Q` at `0x140400d8e`, `f3 0f 59 05 ca f9 7a 00`. This independently fits charge per nominal simulated second. It does not identify that charge unit as joules or the output as watts.
122-
123-This is not a full semantic reconstruction of every argument accepted by `F_FLUID`. For example, the dispatcher forwards a per-vehicle scalar from a parallel list at `0x140549782` to stack argument 7 at `0x1405498b3` [D]. Its meaning is not established here. It is not obtained from the physics worker's elapsed or substep calculation, and the recovered scoop and heat-exchange formulas do not use it as a dt. A decompiler's unnamed float parameter alone is not evidence of a timestep.
