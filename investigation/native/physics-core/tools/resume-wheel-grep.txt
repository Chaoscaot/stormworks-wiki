245:The wiki agent re-read the cited bytes. Definition `+0x2b0`, which the wheel loads at `0x1403acebc` and `0x1403acede`, is bound to the `mass` attribute (`lea r9,[rsi+0x2b0]` at `0x140316ae6`, name string "mass" at `0x140baeaf4`), not to `force_emitter_max_force` (bound to `+0x848` at `0x1403174c4`). The legacy sensor decode reached the same binding independently. A wheel therefore submits its definition mass as shaft weight: 10/20/40/80 for wheels without suspension and tank wheels, 0.7 x mass for wheels with suspension. The `shaft_weight_resource: 1000` field in `wheels.json` is wrong and should be read as the definition mass. Correction by the parent session.
