70:The initializer `0x1403ef450` recalculates state+8 from the tank definition for water types 8 and 20. It does not replace the thirteen amounts or temperature in those tank branches. The component-loading/build window contains virtual serializer calls at `0x1404b8502` and `0x1404b87d4`; the water vtable at `0x140b08f18` resolves slot +0x260 to `0x1403f6150`. Thus this is a native component-state restore path, not just a standalone unused serializer.
72:There is also a distinct reset method `0x1403eebf0`, water virtual slot +0x3c8. It zeros primary and secondary species and totals and sets pending-fill true. It does not zero the temperature fields. A rebuild that preserves the component object or reloads runtime fields must therefore be distinguished from a reset that deliberately clears its inventory. The prior build caller `0x1404bc38a` invokes pending fill after network reconstruction. An already restored false pending-fill byte prevents that operation from refilling the gas tank. A restored true byte allows its existing pending-fill behavior. For diesel/jet fuel, the separate request-gated operation in section 1 still requires its own caller analysis.
