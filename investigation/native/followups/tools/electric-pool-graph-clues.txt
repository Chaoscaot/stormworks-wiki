280:> Motors and both starters use the same draw helper. With infinite_batteries on it returns 1 and removes nothing. Otherwise a missing electrical group returns 0. For a positive request R it removes T = min(a*R, remaining pooled charge) and returns T/R, where a = max(0,(300-n)/300)*q. Here n is the node's EMP countdown, decremented toward zero each normal active update and increased by pulse application. q is the charge fraction published from the shared electrical pool. Low charge, exhausted pooled supply or disconnection can limit draw independently of EMP. The supplied fraction scales motor acceleration. Generators deposit charge regardless of infinite_batteries.
