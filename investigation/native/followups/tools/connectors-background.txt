22:- Hinge, type 2: `magnet_force=0.15`, hinge constraint, center distance strictly less than 0.25 m, plus two endpoint comparisons.
24:- Fluid, type 3; electric, type 4; torque, type 9: center distance strictly less than 0.5 m, point-to-point constraint. The common force pass can be disabled by definition flag 0x100000.
27:The 0.5 m result is deliberate. Small/fluid/electric/torque and hardpoint branches compare squared distance directly with 0.25. At `0x14054e11d`, bytes `f3 41 0f 10 87 78 09 00 00`, the small branch reads that value. `0x14054e126`, `0f 2f c7`, compares it with squared distance, and `0x14054e129`, `0f 86 a8 16 00 00`, rejects equality and greater distances. Large and hinge branches square 0.25 before comparing.
29:For large, hinge and hardpoints, each source sample must find a peer sample with squared separation strictly below `0.25²*0.2`, giving 0.1118034 m. This is a point-matching condition, not a universal angular tolerance. Large uses definition-space points `(0,0,1), (1,0,0), (0,0,-1), (-1,0,0)`; hinge uses `(0,0,-1), (0,0,1)`. The transform scales definition coordinates by 0.25 m and adds `magnet_offset`. Hardpoints start with the same two axial samples and apply definition mirror flags. The center search also checks compatible connector types, eligibility flags, existing links and distinct bodies. These figures do not promise attachment through arbitrary obstructing geometry or to a disabled partner.
