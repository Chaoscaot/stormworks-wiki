51:## 2. General pressure law and fluid indices
52-
53-The existing decoder's state layout applies to tanks, pipe networks and custom compartment fluid states. Relative to a fluid state, +0x08 is V, +0x0c+4*i is species amount, +0x40 is A and +0x44 is temperature. For a custom compartment, the fluid state begins at compartment+0x30.
54-
55-The phase classifier is `0x1402eb550`, retained in `pressure-species.asm`; `0x1402eb5e0` sums liquids and `0x1402eb660` sums gases. Names follow the previously recovered meter layout.
56-
57-| Index | Fluid | Phase |
58-|---:|---|---|
59-| 0 | Fresh water | Liquid |
60-| 1 | Diesel | Liquid |
61-| 2 | Jet fuel | Liquid |
62-| 3 | Legacy air slot | Gas |
63-| 4 | CO2 | Gas |
64-| 5 | Oil | Liquid |
65-| 6 | Seawater | Liquid |
66-| 7 | Steam | Gas |
67-| 8 | Slurry | Liquid |
68-| 9 | Saturated slurry | Liquid |
69-| 10 | Oxygen | Gas |
70-| 11 | Nitrogen | Gas |
71-| 12 | Hydrogen | Gas |
72-
73-Let L=sum of liquid indices, G=sum of gas indices, and A=L+G. For positive V:
74-
75-```
76-W = min(V, 8)
77-if V <= 8:
78-    F = A
79-else:
80-    F = G*min(1, 8/(V-L)) + max(L*4/(60*V), L-V+8)
81-P = 60*F/W
82-```
83-
84-This is the existing window equation, also retained here at `0x1402eb460-0x1402eb4ee`. For example, `0x1402eb4a8-0x1402eb4c6` and adjacent instructions in the excerpt, assemble the gas contribution using the remaining space; the exact sequence is retained to avoid depending on decompiler algebra. Constants 8, 60 and 0.125 are retained in `anchors.json`. `0x1402eb500` computes pressure from this window; pressure calculations in `open-flow.asm` use the same V<=8 split.
85-
86-Consequences, inferred algebraically from those instructions:
87-
88-- Pure gas, every gas index or gas mixture: `P=60*A/V`.
89-- Pure liquid with V<=8: `P=60*A/V`, including the V=4 pipe result `P=15*A` for every liquid, not just water.
90-- Pure liquid with V>8: `P=max(0.5*A/V, 7.5*(A-V+8))`. This is not a universal linear `60*A/V` law. At A=V it gives 60.
91-- Mixed gas and liquid use the full equation. Equal amounts of different liquid species give equal hydraulic pressure. A mixture's temperature does not enter this pressure equation.
92-
93-At degenerate V-L or nonfinite values, the original SSE arithmetic governs behavior. These formulas are for ordinary finite states, not a substitute for its exact NaN behavior. Thermal, combustion and phase-conversion properties can still differ between species.
