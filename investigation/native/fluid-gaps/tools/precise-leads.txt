234-## 7. Filters, mixtures and seawater
235-
236-The legacy `fluid_filter.xml` is water type 10. It enters `0x1403f2394`, shares the valve path, and submits its configured species bit mask from component+0x594. The common two-port call uses bias 0, active factor 1 and one-way false. The constructor defaults the mask to -1 at `0x1403eea09`. The UI in `0x1403f87f0` exposes individual species controls. 237-
238-`fluid_filter_v2.xml` is type 27 and dispatches to `0x1403f262e`. That branch builds a mask from two phase flags. If bit 3 of component+0x594 is set, it enables gas indices 3,4,10,11,12,7. If bit 0 is set, it enables liquid indices 0,6,5,1,2,8,9. The calls to the bit helper `0x14017b3f0` and the final solver call at `0x1403f27b7`, bytes `e8 14 b1 ef ff`, are retained in `filter-new.asm`. Bias is 0, factor 1 and one-way false. Bit3 here means the group flag expanded into all gas indices, not a filter restricted to air slot 3.
239-
240-These masks select moved species. They do not create separate per-species network pressures or remove excluded species from the pressure sum. The existing two-port solver transfers allowed species in proportion to their fraction of source total; a filtered mixture is not equivalent to renormalizing the source to allowed species only.
241-
242-Seawater index 6 is liquid for hydraulics, with the same pressure contribution as fresh water. The prior exterior decode already proved seawater at submerged intakes. A separate phase helper `0x1402eb350`, retained in `phase-change.asm`, converts fresh water plus seawater to steam above 100 C, removing each water type in proportion to its share. Below 100 C it condenses steam into fresh-water index 0. Its limits are `min((100-T)*0.001, 0.1*steam)` below 100, and `min((T-100)*0.001, fresh+sea)` above 100; temperature changes by `0.01*converted/A`. The total amount is unchanged by this helper. Calls at `0x14040ce51`, bytes `e8 fa e4 ed ff`, and `0x14040d5c7`, bytes `e8 84 dd ed ff`, establish selected callers, not a universal every-network phase update. Exact caller/component coverage remains open.
