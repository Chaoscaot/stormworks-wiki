11-## 1. Fixed fluid tank startup, issue #29
12-
13:The constructor starts with zero species amounts and zero total amount. Selection at component+0x588 is diesel index 1, pending-fill at +0x58c is true, fill fraction at +0x590 is 1, and primary temperature at +0x5dc is 20 Celsius. This is the unfilled object state, not the final spawned contents. The gas initializer changes diesel selection 1 to air selection 3 for water type 20. The prior report already traces the non-fuel pending-fill operation `0x1404b7640`.
14-
15:The separate vehicle spawn/resource operation is `0x1405dafa0`. After calling vehicle builder `0x1404bc700`, it enters its resource-fill traversal only when request byte +0x45 is nonzero. The fixed-tank arm specifically requires water type 8 and selection 1 or 2. In the fixed-tank arm at `0x1405dc46e..0x1405dc690`:
16-

18:requested_fuel = 0.99f * fill_fraction * V
19-ambient_air = V / 60.0f
20-if inventory_branch:
21-    fuel_added = min(requested_fuel, inventory[selection])
22-    if fuel_added > 0:
--
29-tank.amount[3] += ambient_air
30-tank.total += ambient_air
31-```
32-
33:The inventory record stores diesel at +0x150 and jet fuel at +0x154, addressed as `+0x14c + 4*selection`. The available-stock comparison also sets an insufficient-resource flag. The native constant at `0x140bafeac` is `a4 70 7d 3f`, binary32 0.9900000095367432; `0x140bb0760` is `00 00 70 42`, or 60.
34-
35:Consequently a cleared tank that reaches the non-inventory arm receives 99% of geometric liquid capacity at fill fraction 1, plus the ambient-air term. A tank that reaches the inventory arm can receive a partial fuel charge, or no diesel/jet fuel when stock is zero. It still receives the ambient-air term. This rules out both "diesel always starts empty because the pending-fill routine skips it" and "diesel always starts full regardless of stock". These are the amounts added by this operation; it does not clear old amounts itself. For the prior decoded small/medium/large geometric capacities, the requested liquid quantities are approximately 30.9375, 185.625 and 696.09375 litres at fill=1, before subsequent exchange.

Do not apply this operation to every spawn or assume it runs when restoring a runtime save. The pending-fill byte is not tested or cleared by this fixed-fuel arm. 54:## 2. Saved compartments and rebuilding, issues #29 and #30
55-
56:Water component serializer `0x1403f6150`, virtual slot +0x260, applies to both fixed fluid tanks and gas tanks. It serializes configuration under context flag 1 and runtime fields under context flag 4, from context+0x10. Runtime state includes the pending-fill Boolean, tagged `spawner_pending`, default true. It then invokes the shared compartment serializer for:
57-
58-- `contained_volume`, component+0x598.
59-- `contained_volume_secondary`, component+0x5e4.
60-
--
62-
63-- `vol_total`: binary32 at state+0x40. For the primary tank this is component+0x5d8.
64-- `temp`: binary32 at state+0x44, primary component+0x5dc.
65-- `rad`: binary32 at state+0x48, primary component+0x5e0.
66:- `vol_0` through `vol_12`: thirteen binary32 species amounts at state+0x0c + 4*i, primary component+0x5a4 + 4*i. These are the same internal amount units used by the prior pressure and content calculations, not an extra save-time conversion to litres.
67-
68:The serializer's text branch uses these names. Its binary branch transfers four bytes per scalar and per species. Temperature validation maps nonfinite input to 0 and bounds finite values to [-273,100000]. Those numbers bound the temperature field, not pressure. Species amounts and `vol_total` have no analogous pressure/rupture threshold in this routine. The geometric volume at state+8 is not among these serialized fields.
69-
70:The initializer `0x1403ef450` recalculates state+8 from the tank definition for water types 8 and 20. It does not replace the thirteen amounts or temperature in those tank branches. The component-loading/build window contains virtual serializer calls at `0x1404b8502` and `0x1404b87d4`; the water vtable at `0x140b08f18` resolves slot +0x260 to `0x1403f6150`. Thus this is a native component-state restore path, not just a standalone unused serializer.
71-
72:There is also a distinct reset method `0x1403eebf0`, water virtual slot +0x3c8. It zeros primary and secondary species and totals and sets pending-fill true. It does not zero the temperature fields. A rebuild that preserves the component object or reloads runtime fields must therefore be distinguished from a reset that deliberately clears its inventory. The prior build caller `0x1404bc38a` invokes pending fill after network reconstruction. An already restored false pending-fill byte prevents that operation from refilling the gas tank. A restored true byte allows its existing pending-fill behavior. For diesel/jet fuel, the separate request-gated operation in section 1 still requires its own caller analysis.
73-
74:Still open: the exact ordering and flag selection for every saved-world, stored-vehicle, editor rebuild and explicit reset entry point. The serializer, constructor defaults, volume initializer, reset method and pending-fill test are recovered, but they do not prove which combination every workflow selects. In particular, no claim is made that all rebuilds preserve contents or that all rebuilds refill tanks. Ruled out: amounts/temperature being absent from native serialization; treating `fluid_fill` as saved live inventory; treating a geometry-volume recalculation as a species reset; treating the explicit reset as temperature restoration.

93:## 3. Pressure safety, issue #30
94-
95:Still open. No maximum safe tank pressure, rupture threshold or pressure-to-damage equation was recovered. This investigation does not establish that pressure damage is impossible.
96-
97:The previously decoded compartment pressure calculation `0x1402eb460` and pressure conversion `0x1402eb500` and fixed-tank update arm `0x1403f3766..0x1403f39cc` were retained as baseline evidence. The gas formula has no maximum-pressure constant in that calculation, and the inspected tank update arm exchanges contents and updates content/pressure outputs without a pressure-triggered rupture branch. The newly traced compartment serializer and fixed-fuel fill arm also do not supply such a threshold. The inspected shipped tank definitions have no maximum-pressure or rupture field.
98-
99:Ruled out as a safety rating: the 59.4-atmosphere cleared gas startup result, the 0.99 initialization multiplier, and the serializer's 100000 temperature bound. These are initialization or temperature values. They cannot be substituted for a tank pressure limit.
100-
101:The complete executable ASCII string/reference search for `pressure|rupture|burst|overpressure` is retained. It finds no ASCII identifier containing rupture, burst or overpressure, but it does find `max pressure` at `0x140b3ede0`, referenced at `0x1408798aa`, and `high_pressure` at `0x140b971d0`, referenced at `0x1405c0114` and `0x1406f9b0a`. Those generic references were not resolved to a tank failure mechanism and are not ruled out globally. Unlabelled comparisons, common vehicle damage code and geometry-compartment failure paths remain untraced. The scoped negative results above are not an executable-wide absence proof.

103-## 4. Microprocessor ports and graph counts, issue #63
104-
105-The editor event dispatcher `0x1407c1b60`, case 0x71, creates an external node. At `0x1407c3eac` it selects the definition at editor+0x118, allocates 0x58 bytes, initializes the node, increments the 32-bit node-ID counter, creates its bridge with `0x14038f150`, grows the node list if capacity equals count, and appends it. There is no comparison against a maximum node count in this Add Input handler. The 0x20 value in list growth is a minimum allocation capacity, not a 32-port cap. This does not prove every other creation/import path has no guard.
