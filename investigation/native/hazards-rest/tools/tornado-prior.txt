84:Vehicles have an extra force path enabled when the weather list contains a flagged tornado entry. For eligible vehicle states, it scales the combined local weather and tornado wind by 7.5 horizontally and 30 vertically, with spatial and vehicle-flag suppression. The result goes through a force-event helper that bypasses the usual connected-assembly mass cap. The producer has no local tornado-distance check, so ordinary wind can enter this path outside the tornado's own wind radius. Physics later applies inverse mass and its integration factor. An identical character path has not been established.
