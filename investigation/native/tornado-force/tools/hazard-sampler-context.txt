214:A useful conditional statement for the wiki is: for an unconstrained body with unit linear factors, a net internal force F held for timestep dt contributes `delta_v = F*dt/body_mass`. At nominal dt=1/60 this is `F/(60*body_mass)`, with a separate torque contribution through inverse inertia. To isolate the tornado for an unchanged body state, evaluate the ordinary surface-force equations with and without the tornado contribution to sampled wind and subtract the resulting forces. The wind sampler's length-100 cap limits that velocity contribution, not force or acceleration. A stationary unshielded surface sees relative air velocity equal to minus the sampled wind; its force still depends on surface weights, normals, density and drag/lift coefficients.
