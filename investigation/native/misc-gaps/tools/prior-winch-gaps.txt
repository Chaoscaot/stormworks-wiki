41-## Current type-48 arithmetic
42-
43-Let C_i be endpoint i's definition capacity, V_i its Speed setting, L the shared rope rest length, and S the sum of both endpoint capacities. The cable object stores L at +0x88. An endpoint stores V at +0xcb0 and its base step at +0xcbc.
--
50-delta_i = s_i * u_i               if abs(u_i) >= 0.1
51-          s_i * (Down_i - Up_i)   otherwise
52-```
53-
54:The speed initializer is `0x1403b4ff0`. `0x1403b5018`, bytes `f3 0f 59 05 84 a8 7f 00`, multiplies definition+0x39c by the float at `0x140baf8a4`, bytes `17 b7 51 39`, approximately 0.0002. `0x1403b5020`, bytes `f3 0f 11 81 bc 0c 00 00`, stores +0xcbc. Constructor stores at `0x1403b406a`, `0x1403b4074`, `0x1403b407e` initialize V=1, lower editor bound=0.1 and upper editor bound=10. `0x1403b44b0` exposes these bounds. `0x1403b45e0` serializes V under `input_velocity`. These are editor bounds; the reel consumer does not re-clamp a loaded V to them.
55-
56:The consumer is the cable-system update `0x140524c80`, particularly `0x140526604` to `0x140526807`. `0x14052660d`, bytes `f3 0f 10 b8 9c 03 00 00`, and `0x140526615`, bytes `f3 0f 58 b9 9c 03 00 00`, form S by adding the definitions' capacities. The digital tests at `0x14052664e` and `0x140526671` read endpoint+0xa40 and +0xa68. A numeric input with magnitude at least 0.1 overrides those digital inputs. Resource node text explicitly calls this numeric input Relative Speed and documents -1 to 1.
57-
58-For ordinary state L <= S, the code accepts a candidate only when it differs from L and satisfies:
59-
60-```
61-0.1 < candidate < S
62-```
63-
64:It does not clamp an overshooting candidate to a boundary. `0x1405266fb` compares candidate against L, `0x140526702` compares against 0.1, and the subsequent comparison against S rejects equality at the upper boundary. Accepted changes set endpoint+0xa90, the movement flag, then submit a rest-length event through `0x1401a8290`. A step can therefore stop short of the nominal capacity. Exact reachable values depend on initial length, step and float rounding.
65-
66:The second endpoint is processed after the first, using the running candidate and shared rope state. Away from boundaries its contribution adds, so two winches can change the shared length at the sum of their rates. Their combined capacity is C_A+C_B. A plain anchor adds no spool capacity. Its resource omits `cable_length`, and the parser supplies zero: `0x140316aed` clears xmm6 and `0x140317321` supplies it as the default for that attribute. This is a shared cable limit, not an independent clamp on two hypothetical cable halves.
67-
68-There is also an explicit L>S recovery branch. It subtracts `s_i * u_i` when the numeric input is active, otherwise s_i. This branch is not the ordinary candidate acceptance rule. It must not be described as an unconditional final clamp to S. Initial cable setup, restored saves and all possible multi-pulley geometry are outside the scalar model's coverage.

70-### Both controls and electricity
71-
72-Digital Down adds s_i and digital Up independently subtracts s_i before acceptance. With both on and Relative Speed inactive, their mathematical net is zero. No changed-length flag is set for an unchanged candidate. A nonzero numeric override still controls movement. The game uses float arithmetic; the equations give nominal arithmetic, not a promise of bitwise cancellation for every stored float.
73-
74:`0x1403b6840` copies the two digital controls to +0xa40 and +0xa68. `0x1403b6c97`, bytes `c6 83 40 0a 00 00 00`, and `0x1403b6c9e`, bytes `c6 83 68 0a 00 00 00`, clear them when the linked-state/electrical-effectiveness checks fail. Those checks do not multiply the reel step by the electrical magnitude or by the charge actually drawn.
75-
76:`0x1403b62b0` requests 0.01 engine charge units when endpoint+0xa90 is set. Call `0x1403b6534`, bytes `e8 67 87 f3 ff`, invokes `0x1402eeca0` on the electrical port at +0x1a8. Its return is discarded for reeling. At full effectiveness and adequate charge, sustained flagged movement requests 0.6 units/s per moving endpoint, independent of Speed. Both digital controls do not create two draw calls in this implementation. A flag from a preceding movement update can still cause a draw; callback timing is not represented by the scalar model.
77-
78:The Relative Speed value at +0xa18 is clamped in `0x1403b6840` and is not cleared with the digital controls. The consumer in `0x140524c80` uses it without another electric-return multiplier or digital gate. Static inference: an active numeric override can change rest length even when the digital path has been disabled for zero electrical effectiveness. This is a code-path finding, not an in-game measurement or a claim about every possible disconnected component state.

80-## Older type-20 arithmetic
81-
82:The actual reel callback is `0x1403e3b30`, vtable slot +0x360. The previously known +0x358 callback, `0x1403e3a70`, only performs fluid exchange. Root+0x148 identifies the linked physical end head. Root+0x498 is the rope wrapper. Reeling requires the wrapper. Component state other than zero suppresses the two controls.
83-
84-Let C be the root's capacity, V be root+0x300, E the shared available charge, and a the electrical effectiveness. The existing electricity decode supplies:
85-
86-```
--
98-if Up:   L = L - 0.004 * V * draw_return()
99-if Down: L = L + 0.004 * V * draw_return()
100-```
101-
102:`0x1403e3c26`, bytes `e8 75 b0 f0 ff`, is the Up draw. `0x1403e3c47`, bytes `f3 0f 59 ce`, multiplies V by 0.004; `0x1403e3c4b`, bytes `f3 0f 59 c8`, multiplies by the draw return; `0x1403e3c4f`, bytes `f3 44 0f 5c d1`, retracts. The Down draw is `0x1403e3c7b`, bytes `e8 20 b0 f0 ff`. `asm/old-reel.asm` and the full function listing retain the surrounding branches.
103-
104:The constant 0.004 is at `0x140baf924`, bytes `6f 12 83 3b`. Constructor `0x140356480` sets V=1 at `0x140356666`, lower editor bound 0.1 at +0x304 and upper bound 5 at +0x308. The Speed editor is `0x140358b30`. `0x1403590c0` persists V as `input_velocity`. These are editor bounds; the reel callback does not re-clamp a loaded V to them. The nominal default rate is 0.24 m/s for every old spool, including Hose. Charge shortage and EMP/effectiveness reduce the corresponding operation's rate through p.
105-
106-A geometric floor precedes the final clamp. The machine arithmetic is:
107-
108-```
--
111-L = max(L, floor)
112-L = min(C, max(0.25, L))
113-```
114-
115:The distance/C division is retained at `0x1403e3e1a`. Its ratio is used directly in this floor; the report does not silently replace it with an intuitive distance-in-metres formula. This is a geometric guard, not a measured force or a tension rating.
116-
117:`0x1403e3f22`, bytes `f3 41 0f 5f d7`, takes the maximum with 0.25. `0x1403e3f27`, bytes `f3 41 0f 5d 97 9c 03 00 00`, takes the minimum with C. `0x1403e3f62`, bytes `e8 29 43 dc ff`, submits the resulting rest length. The Length output copies this scalar at +0x528 to +0x508. Thus the old resource capacities are enforced as inclusive rest-length limits after update. They are not merely tooltip prose. The lower bound can be raised by the geometric guard.

119-### Both controls and electricity
120-
121-Both controls invoke the draw helper twice, in Up-then-Down order. With adequate supply and equal effectiveness, both nominal steps cancel and consume `2*0.01*a` charge units/update. At full effectiveness that is 1.2 units/s. With only 0.015 charge units initially available, Up receives p=1, Down receives p=0.5, and the default-speed net is -0.002 m. This is a retraction bias under shortage, not a general simultaneous-control priority rule.

139:## Rope constraint, stiffness, tension and breakage
140-
141-### Recovered structure
142-
143:The old rope-creation callback `0x1403e3090` and the current cable construction helper `0x140519e30` call `0x1401a5f00`. That helper queues construction through `0x14019d630`. The common constructor creates a Bullet soft body through `0x140159370` and `0x1401462c0`. The installed vtable is `0x140ae3690`, whose RTTI name is `.?AVbtSoftBody@@`. This is a chain of nodes and distance links, rather than a hinge, slider or generic six-axis `btTypedConstraint` with a winch motor impulse cap.
144-
145:`0x140159370` interpolates the nodes and calls `0x140147790` for consecutive links. `0x140147600` gives each link the default material pointer. `0x1401462c0` initializes that material's three double coefficients to 1.0. The constructor additionally writes 5 at soft-body+0x404, approximately 0.01 at +0x368 and 1.0 at +0x3a0, +0x3a8 and +0x3b0. The relevant stores are `0x14019d71f`, `0x14019d72d`, `0x14019d741`, `0x14019d74b`, `0x14019d755`. The exact 0.01 double bit pattern is `0x3f847ae140000000`. These are retained raw solver/configuration settings. A physical spring constant in N/m is not established by these stores alone.
146-
147:The rest-length event vtable `0x140b610f8` has RTTI `.?AUevent_set_object_rope_rest_length@mm_physics_scene@@` and handler `0x1401a1f30`. The handler writes each of n links' rest length as `L/n` and its squared value. `0x1401489e0` contains the same link-length operation used by initial construction. No reel force or torque limit is supplied by these calls.

149:### Additional current-cable impulse
150-
151:There is an explicit corrective impulse in `0x140524c80`, before its reel adjustment. When attachment-point separation D exceeds L, it takes each connected physics body's float at +8. Values below 0.0001 are replaced with 100000. Let the resulting values be K_A and K_B. It computes:
152-
153-```
154-J = min(K_A, K_B) * (D - L)
155:impulse_A = normalize(B - A) * J
156:impulse_B = -impulse_A
157-```
158-
159:It queues the two impulses through `0x1401a7470`. That helper's event vtable `0x140b82d30` has RTTI `.?AUevent_set_impulse@mm_physics_scene@@`. The complete branch is retained in `asm/140524c80.asm` and pseudocode lines around the `D > L` comparison. Interpreting the body+8 value as a mass is an inference here. The recovered fact is the member read and arithmetic. The branch has no explicit cap on J and no deletion/break call. This is one contribution to rope response, not a complete tension model or proof that the whole assembly has unlimited strength.

173-
174:The prior fluids-network result answers the quoted hose pressure-loss gap. Old Hose uses exchange factor 1 at `0x1403e3b09`, bytes `e8 c2 9d f0 ff`. Current rope-hook hose paths use factor 1 or 0.5 according to the path. There is no length term or separate hose storage compartment in those inspected exchange calls. The previous fluid report retains the complete transport equation and its own limits. Reeling length does not enter the decoded bridge factor.
