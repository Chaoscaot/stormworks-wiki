137-## Damage and meltdown
138-
139-The fuel update first heats and relaxes temperature, then compares the result strictly against 1500 C: `0x14041e117: 0f 2f 0d 46 27 79 00`. If hotter, and hot-counter `+0x454` is zero, it calls damage setter slot `+0x20` with `min(damage+1,50)`. It advances that counter modulo 300 only while hot. It does not reset the counter on cooler updates. Consequently, the first hot update damages the rod immediately and later increments occur every 300 accumulated hot updates, five simulated seconds of hot time.
