110-
111-Ammunition damage IDs are 0 kinetic, 1 HE, 2 fragmentation, 3 AP and 4 incendiary. `0x140424120` binds these selector IDs to localization entries 1996 through 2000. See [ammo-properties.asm](native/weapons/asm/ammo-properties.asm) and [ammo-labels.asm](native/weapons/asm/ammo-labels.asm).
112-
113-`0x140593b50` prepares the impact event's magnitude M. Let B be projectile table offset 16 and v its speed at impact:
114-
115-```text
116-kinetic:   M = B*v/1000
117-AP:        M = 0.4*B*v/1000
118-incendiary:M = 0.65*B*v/1000
119-HE:        M = B                  when projectile supports explosives
120-fragment:  M = 0.4*B              when projectile supports explosives
121-```

These are internal magnitudes, not a universal hit-point decrement. 141:At `0x1405928c0`, bytes `ff 90 98 02 00 00`, it calls the component mass getter at virtual slot `+0x298`; `0x1405928d2` reads definition `+0xb8` as the divisor when greater than 1. The base getter `0x140328ea4`, bytes `f3 0f 10 80 b0 02 00 00`, reads definition mass `+0x2b0`; the parser binds that field to `mass`. A separate AP collision path subtracts `200*p`. 143-Impact dispatch is `0x1405947d0`. It calls direct-hit handling `0x140596540` and, for damage IDs 1 or 2, explosion handling `0x140597950`. Explosion support is also checked in the latter. Explosion broadphase radius is `7.5*M`, and its raw radial-force scale is `50000*M`. The routine has a narrower `5*M` radius in one target-specific damage branch. Several branches use `u=max(0,1-distance/radius)` and falloff `u*u*(3-2*u)`; one damage scale is `100*M*falloff`, with separate force and mass caps. Consequently the broadphase number is not a guarantee that every target takes damage at that radius. See [direct-hit.asm](native/weapons/asm/direct-hit.asm) and [explosion.asm](native/weapons/asm/explosion.asm). At `0x140597950` onward, constants `0x140bb0920` and `0x140bb05a0` supply 50000 and 7.5.

This is separate from its reduced explosion magnitude. The final voxel destruction, armour-incidence and blast-occlusion model is still open. The retained work rules out treating B or the table's offset-20 visual-effect value as a single universal damage number.

147-## Timed fuses
148-
149-The gun reads the positive seconds input at component `+0x1a0` only when `damage_id == 2`, or when `damage_id == 1` and gun class exceeds 2. Otherwise fuse time is zero. At `0x140428c50` onward:
150-
151-```text
152-T = max(1/60, input_seconds + 0.02*u - 0.01)  for eligible positive input
153-T = 0                                      otherwise
154-```
155-
156-`u` comes from another 15-bit LCG draw divided by 32767. `0x140428ca5` uses `maxss` against float32 1/60, bytes `f3 0f 5f 0d df 6c 78 00`. The spawned projectile retains T at `+0x4c`; [projectile-spawn.asm](native/weapons/asm/projectile-spawn.asm) shows the copy.
157-
158-At age n, the projectile update tests `T > 0`, `n != 0` and `T <= (n+1)/60`. It computes `a=clamp((T-n/60)/(1/60),0,1)` and queues an impact at `position + a*V_old/60`, then removes the projectile. Thus detonation location is interpolated within an update, while the event is processed on an update. See `0x140592b55` through `0x140592d09` in the projectile update and `0x140593b50`. Lifetime or speed deletion can happen before a long fuse expires. Fragmentation fuse eligibility alone does not override a projectile's explosion-support flag.

160-## Loading and ammunition
161-
162-The ammunition-store path keeps five damage-type bins at component `+0x4fc..+0x50c`. Type-5 stores enforce definition capacity, halving it for projectile ID 8 and dividing it by 5 for ID 9. For the autocannon drums with capacities 100/200/500, this gives light 100/200/500, rotary 50/100/250 and heavy 20/40/100. Resource capacities and all individual parts are retained in JSON.
163-
164-`0x14042ba60` transfers between adjacent parts. A gun or single-round transport slot must be empty with both `+0x330` and `+0x3c4` timers zero. It copies calibre at `+0x3f0`, damage kind at `+0x3f4` and loaded amount at `+0x3f8`. Gun/store calibre mismatches are rejected. Transfers out of a source store decrement one bin; store-to-store movement considers all five bins. The source is emptied after a normal single-round transfer, subject to the native unlimited-ammunition setting branch. Receiver delays are 30, 90 and 240 updates for battle, artillery and Bertha, otherwise 6 for projectile ID 9 and zero for the other gun calibres.
165-
166-`0x14042b870` searches connected neighbours. Junction gating at `0x14042b7e0` recognizes route labels `b1` and `b2` and the junction logic input. In gun update, a spent round at `+0x510` is ejected on the appropriate breech transition by `0x14042b3a0`; automatic-feed guns then request ammunition. The per-group shot counters at `+0x3b8` and table offset 28 limit grouped firing. They are not the heat float at `+0x384`.
167-
168-Still open: a complete direction/order/throughput model for arbitrary belt networks and the tightest firing schedule for externally controlled cannons. The code above rules out instantaneous unrestricted loading, identical capacities across autocannon calibres, and a barrel-length fire-rate multiplier. The numeric breech and receiver delays are recovered; they should not be advertised as a complete cannon RPM measurement.

241-## Reproduction
242-
243-From the repository root:
244-
245-```bash
246-bash investigation/native/weapons/tools/reproduce.sh
247-bash investigation/native/weapons/tools/reproduce.sh --decompile
248-sha256sum -c investigation/native/weapons/SHA256SUMS > /tmp/weapons-sha256-check.txt
249-```
250-
251-The first command verifies the executable hash, extracts definitions and native tables, retains bounded objdump ranges, and regenerates worked examples. The second imports the executable into a fresh `/tmp` Ghidra project with `-noanalysis` and decompiles the listed entry points. It writes its log to `/tmp/weapons-ghidra-reproduce.log`. It never executes the target. The retained `DecompileAt.java` is copied from the supplied update-rate tool. `native.py` imports the supplied read-only `PE` mapper. Python 3.14, GNU objdump and Ghidra 12.1.4 with JDK 21 were used.
252-
253-Some `.pdata` entries describe fragments rather than whole functions, and leaf functions may lack entries. The collector therefore uses explicit ranges in `tools/assembly-ranges.json`. Ghidra prototypes can omit XMM arguments and produce overlapping locals. Assembly, constant bytes and shipped-resource lines take precedence over its variable names. Incorrect exploratory function starts were discarded; the main retained routines were decompiled again in a fresh project.
