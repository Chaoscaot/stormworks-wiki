29-The automatic RPM values are **inferences from the state machine with an immediately available matching round**, not measurements or guaranteed belt throughput. An automatic gun needs an opening and closing transition, plus its load delay: `period = 2*T + delay`, `RPM = 3600/period`. Rotary spin-up is separate: its counter increases toward 30 while not firing and decreases by one per update while firing. Native gun updates are at `0x140427390`; see [gun-update.asm](native/weapons/asm/gun-update.asm), [breech-transition.asm](native/weapons/asm/breech-transition.asm) and [ammo-transfer.asm](native/weapons/asm/ammo-transfer.asm).

143:Impact dispatch is `0x1405947d0`. It calls direct-hit handling `0x140596540` and, for damage IDs 1 or 2, explosion handling `0x140597950`. Explosion support is also checked in the latter. Explosion broadphase radius is `7.5*M`, and its raw radial-force scale is `50000*M`. The routine has a narrower `5*M` radius in one target-specific damage branch. Several branches use `u=max(0,1-distance/radius)` and falloff `u*u*(3-2*u)`; one damage scale is `100*M*falloff`, with separate force and mass caps. Consequently the broadphase number is not a guarantee that every target takes damage at that radius. See [direct-hit.asm](native/weapons/asm/direct-hit.asm) and [explosion.asm](native/weapons/asm/explosion.asm). At `0x140597950` onward, constants `0x140bb0920` and `0x140bb05a0` supply 50000 and 7.5.

158-At age n, the projectile update tests `T > 0`, `n != 0` and `T <= (n+1)/60`. It computes `a=clamp((T-n/60)/(1/60),0,1)` and queues an impact at `position + a*V_old/60`, then removes the projectile. Thus detonation location is interpolated within an update, while the event is processed on an update. See `0x140592b55` through `0x140592d09` in the projectile update and `0x140593b50`. Lifetime or speed deletion can happen before a long fuse expires. Fragmentation fuse eligibility alone does not override a projectile's explosion-support flag.
